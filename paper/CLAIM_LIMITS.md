# Claim limits before submission

Safe descriptions:
- six-wheel rocker-bogie rover simulation
- rigid-body multibody/contact simulation in PyBullet
- spatially varying Coulomb friction
- slip-aware six-wheel torque allocation
- fixed-time disturbance-observer-assisted control
- simulation-based comparative evaluation

Do not claim without extra validation:
- accurate Mars regolith mechanics
- experimentally validated terramechanics
- flight-ready control software
- exact model of Curiosity/Perseverance inertial properties
- exact fixed-time convergence for the full nonlinear contact system
- energy consumption of a real rover battery/motor unless an electrical powertrain model is added

For the paper, call `integral |T omega| dt` an energy or mechanical-work **proxy**, not battery energy.
